This blog is where I share market views and trading notes: option structures such as spreads and butterflies, volatility regimes, risk management, and the psychology of positioning.

I aim to keep the writing clear, structured, and practical. That means:

- breaking complex market moves down into simple narratives;
- recording the reasoning behind each trade, not just the outcome;
- reflecting honestly on both successes and mistakes.

A working knowledge of options will make these notes easier to follow. If you are new to derivatives, these classics are a good place to start:

- John C. Hull, *Options, Futures, and Other Derivatives*
- Sheldon Natenberg, *Option Volatility & Pricing*
- Nassim Nicholas Taleb, *Dynamic Hedging*
- Euan Sinclair, *Volatility Trading*

If you are curious about how I think about options markets and trading discipline, I hope these notes resonate, and perhaps spark a few ideas of your own.
