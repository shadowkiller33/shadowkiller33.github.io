WhenWhat happened
Apr 2021Goes public at US$18 a share, raising US$501.8 million including the underwriters’ option. It calls itself “a biotechnology company scaling more like a technology company”.
Aug 2024Recursion and Exscientia announce a merger, promising about 10 clinical readouts over the following 18 months and US$100 million in operational synergies. Exscientia arrives after a year of pipeline cuts, a CEO departure and a 25% staff reduction.
Nov 2024Layoffs follow within months of the merger. A terminated employee says staff from both the Recursion and the Exscientia side were cut.
May 2025Development halted on two mid-stage programs, for cerebral cavernous malformation and neurofibromatosis type 2, and testing paused on a third, a C. difficile therapy the company will try to license out. The shares fall about 13% on the day.
Jun 2025About 160 people, a fifth of the workforce, are laid off (the company calls it “post-integration employee transitions”) at roughly US$11 million in severance and related costs. R&D is refocused on oncology and rare disease, and the cash runway is extended into the fourth quarter of 2027.
The numbers in early 2025US$509 million in cash at 31 March 2025. A net loss of about US$464 million in 2024, after US$328 million in 2023. Combined pre-merger cash burn of roughly US$606 million in 2024.
Nov 2025Co-founder Chris Gibson steps down as chief executive after 12 years, and Najat Khan, the head of R&D, takes over on 1 January 2026. The same week, an update on REC-617, the CDK7 inhibitor inherited from Exscientia, shows one confirmed response among 29 patients.
Dec 2025First Phase 2 data for REC-4881 in familial adenomatous polyposis: in 12 patients, polyp burden fell by a median of 43% after 12 weeks. The trial has no control group.
Oct 2026Sells US$200 million of new shares. Sanofi takes up its option on the first drug candidate from their collaboration, triggering a US$10 million payment. Recursion says it will look for partners to take forward REC-617 and REC-3565, a MALT1 inhibitor.
Nov 2026Third-quarter revenue of US$18.6 million against a net loss of US$119.0 million. With 15 patients, REC-4881 shows a median 47% reduction in polyp burden, and a registrational trial is planned for the second half of 2027. Fred Hassan, a former pharma chief executive, becomes chair.
The numbers in November 2026About US$749 million in cash after the October offering, which the company says will last into 2029. Cash operating costs capped at US$375 million for 2026. Partners have paid US$216 million (Roche and Genentech) and US$144 million (Sanofi) to date.
