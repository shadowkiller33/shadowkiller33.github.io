WhenWhat happened
Aug 2024Recursion and Exscientia announce a merger, promising about 10 clinical readouts over the following 18 months and US$100 million in operational synergies. Exscientia arrives after a year of pipeline cuts, a CEO departure and a 25% staff reduction.
Nov 2024Layoffs follow within months of the merger. A terminated employee says staff from both the Recursion and the Exscientia side were cut.
May 2025Development halted on two mid-stage programs, for cerebral cavernous malformation and neurofibromatosis type 2, and testing paused on a third, a C. difficile therapy the company will try to license out. The shares fall about 13% on the day.
Jun 2025About 160 people, a fifth of the workforce, are laid off (the company calls it “post-integration employee transitions”) at roughly US$11 million in severance and related costs. R&D is refocused on oncology and rare disease, and the cash runway is extended into the fourth quarter of 2027.
The numbers behind itUS$509 million in cash at 31 March 2025. A net loss of about US$464 million in 2024, after US$328 million in 2023. Combined pre-merger cash burn of roughly US$606 million in 2024.
