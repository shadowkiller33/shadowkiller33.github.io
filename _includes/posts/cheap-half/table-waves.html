Genomics
1992–2012		Patent the genes, and the drugs follow. Human Genome Sciences was founded on sequencing and patenting gene fragments.US$125 million from SmithKline Beecham in 1993, then the 2000 genomics bubble.One approved drug by the time it was sold, twenty years in. A second, the anthrax antibody raxibacumab, cleared the FDA five months after GSK took over. Sold to GSK for US$3.6 billion in 2012. The executive team was replaced within fifteen days. The Rockville site went to Samsung Biologics in 2025 for US$280 million.
Cognitive computing
2012–2022		Watson would read the literature and tell oncologists what to do, and from 2016 Watson for Drug Discovery would find targets for pharma. IBM called Watson Health its moonshot.Billions on the division, plus US$62 million from MD Anderson on one pilot.The oncology pilot was put on hold in late 2016, and a University of Texas audit published in February 2017 found it had never been used on a patient. Drug Discovery sales were halted in April 2019. Watson Health was sold to a private equity firm in 2022 for a reported US$1 billion, against about US$4 billion spent on acquisitions alone.
AI drug discovery, first act
2013–2025		A “bioscience machine brain”. BenevolentAI was valued at US$2 billion in 2018.Europe’s largest SPAC, at up to €1.5 billion, December 2021.Built a pipeline, cut the pipeline, laid off staff in rounds, went back to selling the platform, and delisted from Euronext on 13 March 2025.
